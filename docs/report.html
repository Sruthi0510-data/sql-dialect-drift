What the model cannot see

Remove one decision from the model and re-run. A decision with no confounded partner leaves queries unexplained, which is the tool correctly saying it no longer understands them. A decision with a partner leaves nothing unexplained, because the partner absorbs its work.

So a model with confounded decisions cannot detect that it is missing one of them. Its apparent completeness is not evidence of completeness.
